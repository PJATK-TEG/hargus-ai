**Job Title:** Middle Java Backend Software Engineer

**Job Summary:**

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Java backend software engineer to join our team. The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining our organization's Java-based backend systems, including APIs, microservices, and databases. The Middle Java Backend Software Engineer will work closely with our development team to ensure the delivery of high-quality software solutions that meet our business requirements.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Design, develop, and maintain Java-based backend systems, including APIs, microservices, and databases
* Collaborate with the development team to identify and prioritize project requirements and deliver software solutions that meet business needs
* Write high-quality, efficient, and well-documented Java code to meet project requirements
* Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues and bugs
* Participate in code reviews and contribute to the improvement of the codebase
* Collaborate with the QA team to ensure the delivery of high-quality software solutions
* Stay up-to-date with the latest Java technologies and trends, and apply this knowledge to inform our organization's Java strategy
* Participate in design reviews and contribute to the development of our organization's technical architecture
* Collaborate with the DevOps team to ensure the smooth operation of our backend systems
* Develop and maintain automated testing and deployment scripts using tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, and Docker

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field
* 3-5 years of experience in Java-based backend development, with at least 2 years in a senior role
* Proficiency in Java 8 or higher, including Spring Boot, Hibernate, and Java EE
* Strong understanding of Java architecture, including MVC, DTO, and RESTful APIs
* Experience with Java-based databases, including MySQL, Oracle, and PostgreSQL
* Familiarity with Java-based frameworks, including Spring Framework, Hibernate, and Vaadin
* Experience with Agile development methodologies and version control systems, including Git
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyze complex technical issues and develop effective solutions
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate with stakeholders and team members
* Ability to work independently and as part of a team
* Strong attention to detail and ability to meet deadlines

**Nice to Have:**

* Experience with cloud-based Java platforms, including AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure
* Experience with containerization using Docker
* Experience with automation using tools such as Ansible, Puppet, or Chef
* Experience with Java-based testing frameworks, including JUnit, TestNG, and Cucumber
* Familiarity with Java-based security frameworks, including OAuth, OpenID Connect, and JWT
