**Job Title:** Senior IT Technical Trainer and Project Manager

**About the Role:**

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced IT professional to lead our training and project management efforts. As a Senior IT Technical Trainer and Project Manager, you will be responsible for designing and delivering technical training programs for our employees, as well as managing complex IT projects from start to finish. You will be working closely with our IT team to identify training needs, develop curricula, and deliver training sessions on a variety of technical topics, including programming languages, web development, and IT fundamentals. Your project management skills will be essential in leading cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality projects on time and within budget.

**Responsibilities:**

* Design and deliver technical training programs for employees on a variety of IT topics, including programming languages, web development, and IT fundamentals
* Manage complex IT projects from start to finish, including planning, execution, and delivery
* Collaborate with the IT team to identify training needs and develop curricula to meet those needs
* Develop and maintain relationships with external vendors and partners to ensure timely and effective delivery of training and project services
* Analyze and troubleshoot technical issues related to training and project delivery
* Develop and maintain project schedules, budgets, and resource allocation plans
* Provide expert technical guidance and support to IT staff and employees on technical topics
* Stay up-to-date with emerging technologies and trends in IT and programming languages

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field
* Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field (desirable)
* 5+ years of experience in IT, with a focus on technical training and project management
* Experience with programming languages such as Python, Visual Basic, C#, and Java
* Experience with web development and IT fundamentals
* Project management certification (e.g. PMP, Agile) (desirable)
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
* Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and prioritize multiple tasks and projects
* Experience with training delivery and facilitation (e.g. adult learning, instructional design)
* Experience with technology tools such as Learning Management Systems (LMS), project management software, and collaboration tools

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Experience in a school or educational setting
* Experience with curriculum development and instructional design
* Experience with technology integration in the classroom
* Familiarity with Google and Microsoft suite
* Comptia, Microsoft Technology Associate, or other relevant certifications
