**Job Title:** Senior .NET + Go Developer

**Job Summary:**

We are seeking a highly experienced and skilled Senior .NET + Go Developer to join our team. The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining our organization's software applications, including .NET-based applications and Go-based microservices. The Senior .NET + Go Developer will work closely with our development team to ensure the delivery of high-quality software solutions that meet our business requirements.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Design, develop, and maintain .NET-based applications, including Windows Forms, WPF, and ASP.NET Core
* Design, develop, and maintain Go-based microservices, including Go Web frameworks such as Revel and Gin
* Collaborate with the development team to identify and prioritize project requirements and deliver software solutions that meet business needs
* Write high-quality, efficient, and well-documented code to meet project requirements
* Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues and bugs
* Participate in code reviews and contribute to the improvement of the codebase
* Collaborate with the QA team to ensure the delivery of high-quality software solutions
* Stay up-to-date with the latest .NET and Go technologies and trends, and apply this knowledge to inform our organization's software strategy
* Participate in design reviews and contribute to the development of our organization's technical architecture
* Collaborate with the DevOps team to ensure the smooth operation of our software applications
* Develop and maintain automated testing and deployment scripts using tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, and Docker

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field
* 5-7 years of experience in .NET and Go development, with at least 3 years in a senior role
* Proficiency in .NET Framework 4.6 or higher, including C#, F#, and Visual Basic .NET
* Strong understanding of Go programming language, including Go Web frameworks such as Revel and Gin
* Experience with Go-based microservices, including containerization using Docker
* Familiarity with Agile development methodologies and version control systems, including Git
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyze complex technical issues and develop effective solutions
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate with stakeholders and team members
