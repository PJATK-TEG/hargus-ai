**Job Title:** IT Manager - Building Automation and Cyber Security Specialist

**About the Role:**

We are seeking an experienced IT Manager with a strong background in building automation systems, network engineering, and cyber security to lead our team of engineers and technicians. The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining our enterprise-class IT infrastructure, including building automation systems, network security, and server management. As a key member of our leadership team, you will be responsible for developing and implementing IT strategies, managing budgets, and ensuring the security and compliance of our systems.

**Responsibilities:**

* Manage and maintain the organization's IT infrastructure, including building automation systems, network security, and server management
* Design, implement, and maintain enterprise-class IT solutions, including network infrastructure, server environments, and building automation systems
* Lead cross-functional teams of engineers, technicians, and project coordinators to develop and implement IT projects
* Develop and implement IT strategies, policies, and procedures to ensure the security and compliance of our systems
* Manage budgets, resources, and timelines for IT projects
* Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand their needs and develop solutions that meet their requirements
* Provide technical guidance and support to IT staff and business stakeholders
* Stay up-to-date with the latest technologies, trends, and best practices in IT and building automation systems

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field; Master's degree preferred
* PMP Certification and/or CISSP Certification
* Strong background in building automation systems, network engineering, and cyber security
* Experience with enterprise-class IT infrastructure, including network security, server management, and building automation systems
* Strong leadership and project management skills
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
* Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
* Proficiency in Windows Server, Linux, VMware, Active Directory, Microsoft Exchange, and Cisco technologies
* Experience with building automation systems, including control systems design and implementation
* Strong knowledge of cyber security principles and practices, including risk management, vulnerability assessment, and incident response
* Ability to obtain and maintain necessary security clearances

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Experience with enterprise project lifecycle methodology
* Familiarity with UX design principles and practices
* Knowledge of computer programming languages, such as Python or Java
* Experience with server management, including configuration, maintenance, and troubleshooting
* Familiarity with IT service management frameworks, such as ITIL
* Strong knowledge of IT compliance and regulatory requirements, including HIPAA and PCI-DSS
