**Job Title:** Senior IT Manager and Solutions Architect

**About the Role:**

We are seeking an experienced Senior IT Manager and Solutions Architect to lead our IT initiatives and provide technical expertise to our organization. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in IT management, technical support, and project management. The successful candidate will be responsible for planning, budgeting, and executing IT projects, as well as providing technical guidance and support to our team.

**Responsibilities:**

* Lead and manage IT projects, ensuring timely and within-budget delivery
* Provide technical support and troubleshooting for complex IT issues
* Develop and implement technical solutions to improve IT infrastructure and services
* Manage and maintain our organization's IT systems, including servers, networks, and desktop infrastructure
* Provide technical guidance and support to our team, including training and documentation
* Collaborate with other teams to ensure IT solutions align with business objectives
* Manage and optimize our organization's IT budget
* Stay up-to-date with emerging technologies and trends, and make recommendations for implementation
* Develop and maintain IT policies and procedures
* Ensure compliance with IT security and regulatory requirements

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field
* CompTIA A+ and MCSA certifications (or equivalent)
* Minimum 5 years of experience in IT management, technical support, and project management
* Strong knowledge of IT infrastructure, including networks, servers, and desktop infrastructure
* Experience with cloud computing, virtualization, and IT security
* Excellent communication and project management skills
* Ability to lead and motivate a team
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
* Familiarity with industry-standard software and tools, including Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Suite, and Content Management Systems (CMS)
* Experience with IT project management methodologies, such as Agile or Waterfall

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field
* Experience with web development, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP
* Familiarity with database management systems, including MySQL and SQL Server
* Experience with Linux and Unix operating systems
* Knowledge of IT service management frameworks, such as ITIL
* Experience with cloud-based collaboration tools, such as Slack or Microsoft Teams
