**Job Title:** Director of Cybersecurity and Information Technology

**About the Role:**

We are seeking a highly experienced and skilled Director of Cybersecurity and Information Technology to lead our organization's IT operations and cybersecurity initiatives. The successful candidate will be responsible for overseeing the development and implementation of IT strategies, ensuring the security and integrity of our systems and networks, and providing expert guidance and direction to our team.

**Responsibilities:**

* Develop and implement IT strategies and plans to support the organization's goals and objectives
* Oversee the development and implementation of cybersecurity initiatives, including threat detection, vulnerability management, and incident response
* Provide expert guidance and direction to the IT team on IT operations, cybersecurity, and related matters
* Collaborate with senior leadership to develop and implement policies and procedures related to IT and cybersecurity
* Manage and oversee the development and implementation of new IT projects and initiatives
* Conduct feasibility studies, systems analysis, and systems development to identify opportunities for improvement and optimize IT systems
* Develop and manage budgets for IT and cybersecurity initiatives
* Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to ensure effective communication and coordination of IT and cybersecurity initiatives
* Stay up-to-date with emerging trends and technologies in IT and cybersecurity, and provide recommendations for implementing new technologies and solutions

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field (Master's degree preferred)
* At least 10 years of experience in IT and cybersecurity, with a minimum of 5 years of leadership experience
* Proven track record of success in developing and implementing IT strategies and cybersecurity initiatives
* Expertise in INFOSEC/NETOPS techniques, security management, and budget systems
* Strong knowledge of DoD and U.S. Air Force regulations, principles, and practices pertaining to logistics, INFOSEC/NETOPS, installations, and mission support functions
* Experience with systems integration, systems development, and problem resolution
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively with senior leadership, IT teams, and external stakeholders
* Security clearance: TOP SECRET (SCI) w/CI Polygraph
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to conduct research, summarize results, and make recommendations
* Experience with project management, budgeting, and cost analysis
* Strong technical skills, with expertise in computer operations, configuration management, data management, and software development

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Experience with SCADA systems operations, security, and safeguards
* Knowledge of CCRI processes, procedures, and security practices
* Expertise in capability planning in IT environments, enterprise technical/Certification and Accreditation standards, and IA vulnerability tools
* Experience with Authority to Connect and Authority to Operate network systems
* Strong knowledge of Office of Management and Budget (OMB), Department of Defense (DoD), and U.S. Air Force regulations, principles, and practices pertaining to logistics, INFOSEC/NETOPS, installations, and mission support functions.
